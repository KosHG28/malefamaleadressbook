## Full description (limit 4000)

```
Interlude is a menstrual cycle calendar and a couple's intimacy journal, on one screen.

The cycle is colored by phase: menstruation, follicular, ovulation, luteal. The app works
out the estimated ovulation day, the fertile window and the date of the next period on its
own — and keeps coloring the calendar forward, so you see not only what happened but what
is coming.

WHAT YOU CAN LOG

• Periods — the start, and the end if you want one. A range can be dragged out directly on
  the grid with a finger.
• Intimacy — who started it, orgasms counted for each of you, a note.
• An intimacy proposal — accepted, declined, or still awaiting an answer. A decline can
  carry a reason.
• Masturbation — who, how many, a note.
• Ordinary calendar events.

WHAT THE APP WORKS OUT

• A forecast of the next period, widened by how even your cycle actually is: the more the
  lengths vary, the wider the range. Irregularity is flagged separately.
• Average, shortest and longest cycle length.
• The share of accepted proposals. A logged encounter counts as an accepted proposal on its
  own — most of them were never written down as a proposal beforehand.
• Links between cycle phase and who initiates or who agrees — but only once there are
  genuinely enough observations for it not to be a coincidence.

FOR BOTH OF YOU

Tapping a day opens a note about the current phase — what usually happens to energy and
mood in it. Written plainly and without lecturing: so that the man in the couple
understands what is going on instead of guessing.

THE WHOLE YEAR

A separate screen shows all twelve months as a mosaic — phase colors and the same marker
rings the main calendar uses. Tapping a month jumps to it.

PRIVACY

The most important part of an app like this:

• Every entry is stored only on your phone.
• The app has no internet permission. It physically cannot send anything anywhere.
• No account, no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no trackers.
• The developer does not see your data and cannot.
• The app itself can be locked with a fingerprint or the phone's own passcode.
• Every entry exports to a file and imports back — the data stays yours.

APPEARANCE

Five color schemes, light and dark themes, and an adaptive theme that shifts the interface
tint along with the cycle phase. Marker colors and legend visibility are configurable. The
home-screen icon follows the scheme you pick.

LANGUAGES

Russian and English. The app follows the system language; to set it separately, use
Android's own per-app language picker in system settings.

IMPORTANT

Interlude is not a medical device and does not replace a doctor. The ovulation and fertile
window forecast is calendar-based and is not suitable as contraception.
```
